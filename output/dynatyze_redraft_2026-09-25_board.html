Tiers

ALL groups the whole field by overall score. A position tab groups only the players eligible there, so you can see how deep each position runs.

Major cliffTier breakNot draftedChip: rank, player, auction value

Position scarcity

Last starter is the score of the last player a league fills that position's starting slots with (G and PF/C split between their positions); a low number means good players there run out first, and that position's players gain value. Replacement is the average score of the best three players outside the core. Tiers split wherever the drop to the next player is at least twice the typical drop around it. Major cliffs are drops that are large for the position as a whole.
