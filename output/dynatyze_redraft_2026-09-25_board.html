Tiers

ALL groups the whole field by overall score. A position tab groups only the players eligible there, so you can see how deep each position runs.

Position scarcity

Replacement is the average score of the best three undrafted players at the position. Tiers split wherever the drop to the next player is at least twice the typical drop around it. Major cliffs are drops that are large for the position as a whole.

    Market bargains

    Core players where the market's average auction price differs most from our value. Bargains stretch your budget; overpriced players are the ones to let someone else buy. * means the player isn't in the market list, so he usually goes for the minimum. Market prices come from ESPN leagues in general, not yours, and have no games cap.

    Where to spend

    Field tiers inside the core pool. Pay up: a major cliff follows, so nobody later replaces them. Deep: wait: five or more near-equal players, so one will go at or under value once budgets thin out.
